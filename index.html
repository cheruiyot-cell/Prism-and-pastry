Frequently Asked Questions

Still have questions? We've answered the most common ones below – or just WhatsApp us anytime.

Delivery & On‑Time Guarantee

If we're more than 30 minutes late, you get 10% off your next order. If your cake arrives damaged, we'll remake it free – no arguments, no stress.

We'll call or text you when the rider is close. If you can't come to the gate, you can authorise us to leave the cake with a trusted neighbour, the estate security, or the office reception. We'll take a photo as proof of handover.

No problem. Just WhatsApp us as soon as possible, and we'll find a new time slot that works for you. For redelivery after a failed attempt, a small delivery fee may apply – but we'll always confirm with you first.

Payment & M‑Pesa

Yes! We accept M-Pesa, bank transfer, and cash on delivery for orders within Nairobi. For custom orders, a 70% deposit is required to confirm your booking, with the balance due 24 hours before pickup or delivery.

We understand. For orders placed more than a month in advance, you can pay a 60% deposit and the remaining 40% 24 hours before your event. We want to make things flexible for you.

We track every M-Pesa transaction with a unique receipt number so nothing gets lost. If a payment ever fails or shows "pending," our team will sort it out within minutes so you're never left wondering.

Custom Orders

For wedding cakes or very detailed designs, please give us at least 2 weeks' notice. For standard birthday cakes, 3–5 days is usually enough. But we can often do same-day for cakes in our ready-made collection – just WhatsApp us to check.

Don't worry! Tell us your budget, and we'll adjust the size, design, and details to make it work. We've never met a cake idea we couldn't simplify beautifully – you'll still get a stunning cake without breaking the bank.

Yes! Chat with us on WhatsApp, and we'll send you a link to our portfolio of past cakes, wedding cake designs, and birthday masterpieces. See what we've made for other happy customers before you commit.

Taste & Quality

We're known for our Real Taste Guarantee. We bake in small batches daily, using real butter, Belgian chocolate, and fresh cream – no preservatives, no dry cardboard. If your cake doesn't taste amazing, we'll remake it free.

Yes! We offer free tasting sessions at our Imara Daima studio. Come sample our flavours, meet our pastry chef, and decide on your perfect combination before you commit.

Pickup & Storage

Place your cake on a flat surface – the floor of the front passenger seat is perfect. Keep the aircon on if it's a hot day, drive slowly over bumps, and don't rush. Your cake should sit on a flat surface, not on your lap.

Store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. On hot days, wrap it tightly and keep it in the fridge. Remove it about 2 hours before serving so it comes to room temperature – that's when the flavours are at their best.

Dietary Options

Yes! We can prepare gluten-free, sugar-free, and eggless options upon request. Just tell us at the time of ordering, and we'll make sure everyone at your event can enjoy a slice.

Pricing & Payment

Imara Daima and South B — free. CBD, Westlands, Kilimani, Karen — KES 300–500. Kiambu and Machakos — quoted on WhatsApp before you pay. You'll always know the exact number in writing before you send anything.

50% deposit locks your date. Balance on delivery or pickup. For orders 30+ days out, we can split it into three payments if that helps.

Every M-Pesa payment gets a receipt number logged against your order. If we ever fail to deliver, you get 100% of your deposit back within 24 hours — in writing, no arguments.

Our Guarantees

More than 30 minutes past your window? The cake is free — not discounted, free. Damaged in transit? We bake a replacement the same day, at our cost.

Taste & Quality

We're known for our Real Taste Guarantee. We bake in small batches daily, using real butter, Belgian chocolate, and fresh cream — no preservatives, no dry cardboard. If your cake doesn't taste amazing, we'll remake it free.

Yes! We offer free tasting sessions at our Imara Daima studio. Come sample our flavours, meet our pastry chef, and decide on your perfect combination before you commit.

Pickup & Storage

Place your cake on a flat surface — the floor of the front passenger seat is perfect. Keep the aircon on if it's a hot day, drive slowly over bumps, and don't rush.

Store it in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. On hot days, wrap it tightly and keep it in the fridge. Remove it about 2 hours before serving so it comes to room temperature.

Dietary Options

Yes! We can prepare gluten-free, sugar-free, and eggless options upon request. Just tell us at the time of ordering.
